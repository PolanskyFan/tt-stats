Paste a draw

Paste the whole tournament post if you like — singles and doubles, qualifying and main draw, in one go. On Auto the discipline comes from headings like "DOUBLES DRAW AND RESULTS" and the stage from the round itself, since QR1 and QFR can only be qualifying. Winners are read from the bracket.

Paste a ranking list

The forum post, dot leaders and all. The week name is picked up from the title line if it's there. Once loaded, every draw you tag with that week gets both rank columns filled in automatically.

Tournament calendar

Paste a season's calendar straight from the sheet — tab separated, month headings and repeated header rows are skipped. It gives every tournament its date, country, surface, draw size and manager, which in turn drives the calendar page, manager credits, surface records and the draw-size check. Pasting a season again replaces it.
